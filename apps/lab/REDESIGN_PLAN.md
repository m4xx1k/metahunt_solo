# Lab redesign — "or" vs "and" from anyOf groups

**Read this file completely before touching anything. Then do the phases in order.**

## What this is

Vacancy extraction now records alternatives: `"AWS or Azure"` is stored as one
requirement whose skills share a `requirement_group`. The lab graph still treats every
pair of skills as "asked for together", so it tells people to learn both AWS and Azure.
This redesign makes the lab split every pair into:

- **substitute** — the posting offered them as alternatives ("A or B")
- **complement** — the posting required both ("A and B")

The pipeline and the check scripts are **already written and verified** against the lab
database. They are in `apps/lab/redesign/files/`. Your job is to copy them in, then
update the TypeScript types, three views, and the copy/docs, exactly as specified.
