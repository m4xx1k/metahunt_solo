## Subtasks

- [x] T1 — Make the lab’s reading contract discoverable — *done when:* Map is the default, labels can be toggled, every view explains itself, and one FAQ owns shared terms.
- [ ] T2 — Hand off the dossier as the evidence layer for position archetypes — *done when:* its data boundaries and the MET-24 dependency are recorded before a position-grain clustering pipeline is proposed.

## Decisions

- Dossier uses only the committed graph artifact and hand-reviewed pair labels. It does not promote REQUIRED-derived counts to validated user advice while MET-24 is in progress.
- “Often” means co-requested in at least 60% of positions containing the selected skill; “sometimes” is 20–59%. These are presentation buckets, not new data classes.
